## Summary

PlaySteps is designed to work offline. Everything you record about your child is
stored on your own device. We operate no analytics, no advertising, and no
third-party tracking of any kind.

Data leaves your device only if you explicitly turn on Family Sharing, and only
to the backend the app is configured to use.

---

## What the app stores

All of the following is written to a database on your device:

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Child's name or nickname | Personalises activities and reminders |
| Date of birth | Selects age-appropriate activities and milestones |
| Milestone achievements and your notes | Milestone tracking |
| Activity completions and streaks | Daily progress and badges |
| Growth measurements (weight, height, head circumference) | Growth charts |
| Photo memories you capture | Your memories timeline |
| Reminder time and app preferences | App settings |
| Purchase status (whether Premium is unlocked) | Unlocks paid features |

We do not ask for, and the app does not collect, your name, email address,
phone number, precise location, contacts, or device advertising identifiers.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Camera** — only when you choose to attach a photo to an activity or
  milestone. Photos are saved on your device.
- **Notifications** — only to deliver the daily play reminder you schedule.
  You can turn reminders off at any time in Settings.

Denying either permission leaves the rest of the app fully usable.

## Family Sharing (optional, off by default)

If you create an account and enable Family Sharing, the following is uploaded to
the app's backend so a co-parent can see the same information: child profile
details, activity completions, milestone achievements, unlocked badges, and
growth measurements.

**Photo memories are not synchronised at all.** Neither the image nor its
caption leaves the device that captured it, so a photo is only ever visible on
that device.

If you never enable Family Sharing, the app makes no network requests for your
data and no account is created.

## Analytics and advertising

There are none. PlaySteps contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no
advertising SDK, and no social-network SDK. We do not profile you or your child,
and we do not sell or share personal information with anyone.

## Purchases

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or
receive your payment details. The stores tell the app only whether a purchase is
valid.

## Children's privacy

PlaySteps is a tool **for parents and caregivers**, not a service directed at
children, and it is not intended to be used by a child.

Information about a child is entered by the adult who owns the device and is
kept on that device. Because we do not operate a service directed at children
and do not collect personal information from children online, we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children under 13.

> **For counsel:** the app stores a child's name, date of birth, photographs, and

> the GDPR (including Article 8 and any special-category analysis of growth

## How long data is kept

On-device data is kept until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
Uninstalling removes the local database.

If you used Family Sharing, use **Settings → Leave family** to stop syncing, and
contact us at the address above to request deletion of synced records.

## Your rights

You can access and delete everything the app holds directly in the app:

- Delete a child profile in **Settings → Children**.
- Export a full copy of your data in **Settings → Backup**.
- Turn off reminders in **Settings → Notifications**.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct,
export, or erase personal information, and to lodge a complaint with your data
protection authority. Contact us at the address above to exercise them.

## Security

On-device data is protected by your device's own security (passcode, biometrics,
and OS-level app sandboxing). If you enable Family Sharing, data in transit is
encrypted with HTTPS.

No system is perfectly secure, and data stored on a device that is unlocked,
jailbroken, or rooted may be accessible to other software on that device.
